What Is Pizza Made of?

Pizza has multiple ingredients and classical recipies include:

  1. Cheese(commonly mozzarella).
  2. Dough, which is used for the base and crust of the pizza.
  3. Tomato sauce is the last essential part of a traditional pizza.

    4. However, more modern recipies have begun to substitute tomato sauce for others, such as barbecue sauce.
