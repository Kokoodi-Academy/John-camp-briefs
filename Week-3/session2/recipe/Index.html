Classic Pepperoni Pizza

A crispy, cheesy homemade pizza topped with rich tomato sauce and spicy pepperoni.

Preparation Time

Prep: 25 minutes | Rise: 1 hour | Cook: 12-15 minutes | Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 500g strong bread flour
  • 7g fast-action dried yeast
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 325ml warm water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 200g pizza sauce or passata
  • 250g grated mozzarella
  • 100g sliced pepperoni
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar in a large bowl.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil, then stir until a dough forms.
  3. Knead for 8-10 minutes until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  4. Cover the bowl and leave the dough to rise for 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
  5. Heat the oven to 240°C (220°C fan) and place a baking tray inside to warm.
  6. Divide the dough into two portions and stretch each into a thin 25cm circle.
  7. Spread pizza sauce over each base, leaving a small border.
  8. Add the mozzarella, pepperoni, and oregano.
  9. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  10. Rest for 2 minutes, slice, and serve hot.
